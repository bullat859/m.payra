01 Introduction & Architecture

This Privacy Policy describes how Dynamic Edge Gesture Control (Application ID: com.m.payra.gesture.control.app), developed by Madhusudan Payra ("we", "us", or "the developer"), collects, utilizes, and safeguards your information.

The application provides system customization, edge navigation sliders, notch menus, floating panels, and situational shortcuts. The app operates on a strict local-first processing model: your gesture triggers, touch detection routines, custom shortcuts, and personal notes are computed and retained directly on your device. We run no external user-profiling servers and never harvest or sell your personal files.

By using Dynamic Edge Gesture Control, you acknowledge and agree to the permissions model, hardware usage, and third-party integrations outlined below.

02 Complete Permissions Disclosure

To deliver system-wide gestures and overlay automation, our application requests explicit permissions. Each is strictly scoped to features you configure:

🛠️ Accessibility Service API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Core Required Permission

The Accessibility Service API is the foundational engine of Dynamic Edge Gesture Control. It is strictly utilized to:

  • Draw touch-sensitive gesture overlay handles (edge sliders, notches, dynamic islands, and pie menus) on top of active screens.
  • Detect your swipe, tap, long-press, and drag gestures specifically within these configured edge handle zones.
  • Execute global Android system actions assigned to your gestures (e.g., Back, Home, Recent Apps, Quick Settings, Split Screen, and Power Menu).
Strict Accessibility Privacy Guarantee: We do NOT use the Accessibility Service API to read screen content, inspect keystrokes, intercept passwords, or log personal messages.

⚙️ Foreground Services & Lifecycle Management

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps the gesture engine alive in background memory so touch handles remain responsive without being terminated by OS memory reclamation.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA: Required by Android 14+ when triggering flashlight or camera gesture tools while running as an active background service.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE: Required by Android 14+ when the user binds an edge gesture to capture instant voice memos.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Powers the offline media widget and notch playback controls when music is active.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Re-initializes your active edge touch zones and floating panels automatically after device restarts.
  • WAKE_LOCK & REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Prevents deep sleep routines from disconnecting edge handle sensors and allows optional battery optimization exclusions for smooth responsiveness.

🪟 Overlays, Haptics & System Controls

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Renders floating gesture indicators, dynamic notch menus, and pie overlays over third-party applications. This permission only displays UI and cannot inspect or record what is beneath the overlay.
  • WRITE_SETTINGS: Allows swipe actions to toggle system brightness, volume, auto-rotate locks, and display timeouts.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS & VIBRATE: Modifies audio stream levels directly and generates tactile haptic pulses on edge swipe completion.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT & BLUETOOTH_SCAN: Toggles device Bluetooth states and connects to paired headsets or accessories directly from quick gesture menus.

📍 Location Services (Foreground & Background)

Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION.

Used exclusively for Situational Geofenced Triggers (e.g., auto-disabling edge triggers when entering a workplace). Geofence events are evaluated entirely on your device and are never sent to external servers.

🔔 Notifications, DND & Alarms

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Posts mandatory foreground service notices and displays media controls.
  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY: Enables mapped gestures to toggle Do Not Disturb (DND) modes.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM: Allows precision scheduling of profile switches or reminder timers triggered by gesture shortcuts.

📸 Camera, Microphone, Storage & Screen Capture

  • CAMERA: Used on demand to toggle the device flashlight and capture quick silent photo/video records triggered by your mapped gestures.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Activated only when executing a voice recording action assigned to an edge gesture.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used to load local music for the audio controller widget, import custom icon images, and save/export backup configuration files.
  • LAUNCH_CAPTURE_CONTENT_ACTIVITY_ALLOWED: Facilitates native Android screen capture intent invocations directly via configured gesture swipes.
Local Storage Privacy Guarantee: All photos, audio memos, and videos captured through shortcut triggers are saved directly into your device's private or media storage. They never leave your device.

📞 Direct Dial, Contacts & Biometrics

  • CALL_PHONE & READ_CONTACTS: Allows assigning direct speed-dial actions to edge swipes. Contacts are accessed strictly to let you select numbers inside the app's configuration UI.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC & USE_FINGERPRINT: Protects app settings and private in-app notes. Biometric verification occurs within Android's hardware security enclave; raw biometric templates are never accessible to our app.

🛡️ Internal Broadcast Security

com.m.payra.gesture.control.app.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: An application-level signature permission ensuring that internal broadcast communications between the app's components cannot be intercepted by third-party apps installed on the device.

03 Device Hardware Features & Compatibility

Our application declares specific hardware dependencies in its manifest via <uses-feature> elements. These specify the physical capabilities used to support mapped gesture actions:

• Telephony (android.hardware.telephony): Enables speed-dial and emergency call gesture shortcuts.

• Camera & Autofocus (android.hardware.camera, camera.autofocus): Supports flashlight controls and quick photo capture triggers.

• Audio Input (android.hardware.microphone): Enables voice recording shortcuts.

• Bluetooth & BLE (android.hardware.bluetooth, bluetooth_le): Toggles hardware radios and manages accessory connections.

• Location Sensors (android.hardware.location, location.gps, location.network): Powers situational geofence profile triggers.

• Touch Emulation (android.hardware.faketouch): Ensures basic drag and swipe gesture recognition functions across a wider variety of touch screens and simulated pointers.

Compatibility Note: Hardware features are utilized conditionally. If your device lacks specific hardware (such as cellular calling on a Wi-Fi-only tablet), those actions are gracefully disabled while all other gestures remain fully operational.

04 Package Visibility (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Policy)

Dynamic Edge Gesture Control declares the android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. In accordance with Google Play's High-Risk Permission policy, this permission is strictly required for the core functionality of the application:

  • Launcher & App Launch Gestures: Allows you to map any installed app to a swipe gesture, notch menu item, floating bubble dock, or radial pie menu.
  • Per-App Gesture Blacklists / Exclusion Profiles: Allows the app to detect when specific foreground apps (such as camera viewfinders, full-screen video games, or drawing canvases) are opened, automatically suppressing edge triggers to avoid accidental touches.
Package Visibility Privacy Commitment: The inventory of installed applications is processed strictly on your local hardware. We do not catalog, transmit, or monetize the list of applications installed on your device.

07 Data Retention, Deletion, & Security

Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and Firebase / AdMob endpoints use secure TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols.

Data Retention & Deletion: Local app configurations, custom note files, and captured media stay on your device until you:

  • Delete individual triggers, media items, or notes inside the app;
  • Clear app storage in Settings → Apps → Dynamic Edge Gesture Control → Storage → Clear Data; or
  • Uninstall the app, which automatically wipes the app's local sandbox storage.
